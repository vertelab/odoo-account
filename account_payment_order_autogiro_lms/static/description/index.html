Account: Payment Order & Autogiro Training (LMS)

Swedish training on the four vendor-bill payment paths in Odoo 18.

Odoo 18.0· Accounting/Training· by Vertel Sverige AB

Overview

Publishes a Swedish training course on how paying a vendor bill actually behaves in Odoo 18. The four payment paths look nearly identical in the UI but leave the bill in different states — which is what the course exists to explain.

Without that distinction the year-end close goes wrong: a bill that looks paid may still read Pågående, meaning the money has not left the account and a bank reconciliation is still outstanding.

Typical use: Use it to teach accounting staff to tell Betald from Pågående and to know when a bank reconciliation is required.

The Four Payment Paths

PathHowWhat is postedBill outcome
aPayment order, method without the pending flagPayment posted and reconciled at uploadBetald immediately
bPayment order, method with the pending flagNothing posted at uploadPågående until bank reconciliation
cManual payment (Pay), method without the pending flagPayment posted immediately with the chosen dateBetald immediately
dManual payment (Pay), method with the pending flag (exception)Nothing — no payment is created at allPågående until bank reconciliation

Paths a and c post immediately and yield Betald. Paths b and d wait for the bank and yield Pågående. The difference between a/b and c/d is only how the payment is started — payment order or the Pay button.

What decides is the flag, not the name. A method's Pending Until Reconciliation setting (Accounting → Configuration → Payment Methods) determines whether it takes path a or b. Autogiro is the common case, but IBAN, Bankgiro and SEPA can carry the flag too — so a payment that looks "ordinary" may still wait for the bank. The flag is per method, so different methods in the same company can behave differently.

Key Features

Four sections, 22 slides

Overview and decision guide, payment orders, manual payment, and year-end close with troubleshooting.

Quiz per section

Five questions per section with an explanation on every answer.

Verified behaviour
